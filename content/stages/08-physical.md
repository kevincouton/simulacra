# Stage 8 — The Physical World (2026, in progress)

The last row of the grid never quite turns red — and that's the point.

- **Poolside's reverse-execuhire letter** — the world's problems split into
  intelligence-bound ones (solvable by scaling cognition, soon commoditized
  by open weights) and experiment-bound ones, where "no amount of
  intelligence substitutes for real-world experimental feedback — 100,000
  brilliant minds won't cure cancer without a wet lab." Their bet: AI's
  durable value accrues to whoever owns the experimental loop — AI as "the
  world's most valuable scientific discovery engine."
- **CZ Biohub** — imaging the Human Cell Atlas into a virtual cell, because
  in silico is roughly 1000x cheaper and faster than in vivo, and extending
  toward a virtual immune system.
- **Chai, Xaira, Lila** — data-center-shaped labs filling in the AI-for-
  science stack.

The physical world is the one component that can't be fully synthesized —
only compressed, cell by cell, into models.
